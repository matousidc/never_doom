Never Doom

Replace the fear of rejection with the fear of not living up to your full potential. Make sure that, on your deathbed, you will think highly of yourself. Take care and focus on yourself instead of trying to be liked by people who don't even like themselves.
You know deep down you could be better then you are now, BUT YOU MUST ACT. You know you can accomplish great feats, BUT YOU MUST ACT. You know you could be everything you ever dreamed of being, BUT YOU MUST ACT. NEVER DOOM.
Opportunities don’t wait until you’re ready. Miss them now and you miss them forever.
You can achieve anything you set your mind to.
